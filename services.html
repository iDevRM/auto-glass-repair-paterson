Windshield Replacement in Paterson

A new windshield fixes long cracks, damage in front of the driver, cracks that reach the edge and glass that’s been sandblasted by a winter of road grit. It brings back the glass that helps hold the roof up and backs up the passenger airbag.

Anyone with a crack longer than a dollar bill, or a break right in their line of sight, needs one. Leave it and every cold night and hot defroster blast pushes the crack further, and a Paterson officer can ticket a windshield that blocks your view.

Windshield Chip Repair in Paterson

Chip repair fills a small break with clear resin, cures it with UV light and stops it from spreading. Your original glass and factory seal stay in place.

Drivers with a chip smaller than a quarter, or a short single crack, should call within a week or two. Wait through a few freezing nights and the chip can split into a crack that only a new windshield fixes.

ADAS Camera Calibration in Paterson

ADAS calibration lines up the forward camera behind your windshield so lane-keeping, adaptive cruise and automatic braking read the road the way they should.

Most cars from the last several years with a camera near the rearview mirror need it after a new windshield. Skip it and you might see warning lights, or a system that steers or brakes a little off target.
